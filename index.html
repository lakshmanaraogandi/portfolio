    About Me

    I am an MSc Aerospace Engineering student at TU Darmstadt with a strong interest in computational fluid dynamics, CAE simulation and engineering automation.

    During my Master's thesis at SICK AG, I worked on the numerical investigation of disturbed internal gas flows and flow conditioning. My work involved more than 100 OpenFOAM RANS simulations, CFD pre-processing, mesh generation, post-processing and experimental flow measurements.

    I also developed Python-based automation workflows for simulation setup, case preparation, solver execution, post-processing and data analysis. This gave me practical experience combining engineering simulation with programming and Linux-based workflows.

    My broader experience includes thermo-fluid simulations, turbomachinery CFD, heat transfer, CAD, FEM and experimental validation using tools such as OpenFOAM, STAR-CCM+, ANSYS Fluent and ANSYS CFX.
