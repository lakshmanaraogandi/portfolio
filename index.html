About Me

I am a Master's student in Aerospace Engineering at TU Darmstadt with hands-on experience in computational fluid dynamics, CAE simulation, numerical analysis, CAD, FEM and experimental validation.

My experience includes OpenFOAM, STAR-CCM+, ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS CFX and ANSYS Mechanical, with projects covering internal flows, turbomachinery, thermo-fluid systems, aerodynamics, heat transfer and structural analysis.

During my Master's thesis at SICK AG, I performed more than 100 RANS simulations of disturbed internal gas flows and developed a Python-based automated CFD workflow covering case setup, meshing, simulation execution and post-processing. I also worked with experimental flow measurements to compare and validate the numerical results.

I am particularly interested in combining engineering simulation, automation, data analysis and software-based process improvement to develop efficient and reliable engineering workflows.
